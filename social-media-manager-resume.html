Social media manager resume example and tips

A social media manager resume works best when it shows results, not only tasks. Use real numbers for growth, reach and engagement, and link to pages you have managed.

Build your resume free

What to include

  • Contact details and a link to your portfolio or best pages
  • A summary naming the platforms and types of businesses you have worked with
  • Experience lines about planning, posting, design, replies and reporting
  • Tools such as Canva, Meta Business Suite and scheduling apps
  • Results with numbers, such as follower growth, reach or sales leads

Sample summary

Creative social media manager who plans content, grows communities and turns engagement into customers. Experienced with small business pages and simple monthly reports.

Replace anything in [brackets] with your own facts and numbers. Only write what is true.

Sample achievement lines

  • Planned and scheduled [number] posts a week across Facebook and Instagram
  • Designed graphics and short videos in Canva that raised engagement by [number]%
  • Replied to messages and comments to keep the page active and friendly
  • Tracked reach and engagement in a monthly report with next-step ideas

Skills to consider

Content planning, Canva, Meta Business Suite, Copywriting, Community management, Basic video editing, Analytics, Customer replies

Quick tips

  • Keep it to one page when you can.
  • Start each line with an action word, such as managed, prepared or reduced.
  • Copy useful words from the job ad into your summary and skills, as long as they describe you.
  • Save it as a PDF so the layout stays the same on every device.
