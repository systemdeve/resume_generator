Virtual assistant resume example and tips

Clients hiring a virtual assistant want proof that you are reliable, organized and easy to work with from far away. Show the tasks you can take off their plate and the tools you already know.

Build your resume free

What to include

  • Contact details, time zone and the hours you can work
  • A summary that says who you support and how you help
  • Tasks you handled: email, calendar, travel, data entry, customer support and reports
  • Tools such as Google Workspace, Zoom, Notion, Trello or Slack
  • Short examples of work or a link to your portfolio

Sample summary

Organized and proactive virtual assistant who keeps calendars, inboxes and projects running smoothly for busy clients. Comfortable working across time zones and quick to learn new tools.

Replace anything in [brackets] with your own facts and numbers. Only write what is true.

Sample achievement lines

  • Managed email, calendar and travel for [number] executives
  • Prepared reports, meeting notes and presentations on tight deadlines
  • Set up task boards and routines that saved the team time each week
  • Answered customer messages within [time] during working hours

Skills to consider

Calendar management, Email management, Google Workspace, Notion, Trello, Zoom, Data entry, Customer support, Time management

Quick tips

  • Keep it to one page when you can.
  • Start each line with an action word, such as managed, prepared or reduced.
  • Copy useful words from the job ad into your summary and skills, as long as they describe you.
  • Save it as a PDF so the layout stays the same on every device.
