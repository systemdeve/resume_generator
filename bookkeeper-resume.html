Bookkeeper resume example and tips

A bookkeeper resume should show that you are accurate, organized and dependable with money records. Hiring managers look for the tasks you handled, the software you know and how your work helped the business.

Build your resume free

What to include

  • Your contact details and location
  • A short summary with your years of experience and main strengths
  • Experience lines about accounts payable, accounts receivable, bank reconciliation and month-end reports
  • Software you use, such as QuickBooks, Xero and Excel
  • Certificates, for example Certified Bookkeeper (CB), and your education

Sample summary

Detail-oriented bookkeeper with [number] years of experience in accounts payable, accounts receivable and bank reconciliation. Skilled in QuickBooks and Excel, and committed to accurate, on-time monthly reports.

Replace anything in [brackets] with your own facts and numbers. Only write what is true.

Sample achievement lines

  • Reconciled bank and credit card accounts every month and cleared all differences
  • Processed [number] invoices a month and followed up on overdue payments
  • Prepared monthly profit and loss and balance sheet reports for the owner
  • Organized records so tax filing documents were ready on time

Skills to consider

Bookkeeping, Accounts payable, Accounts receivable, Bank reconciliation, QuickBooks, Xero, Excel, Payroll basics, Month-end closing

Quick tips

  • Keep it to one page when you can.
  • Start each line with an action word, such as managed, prepared or reduced.
  • Copy useful words from the job ad into your summary and skills, as long as they describe you.
  • Save it as a PDF so the layout stays the same on every device.
