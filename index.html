Free online resume maker

Resume Maker is a free resume builder and CV maker that works in your browser. Choose a resume template, pick a color theme and font, add your photo, and see your resume update as you type. When you are done, download it as a PDF or print it. You do not need to sign up, and your details stay on your own device.

Resume templates for common jobs

Start from a ready-made template for a bookkeeper, virtual assistant, social media manager or fresh graduate, or use a plain ATS-friendly resume that applicant-screening software can read easily. You can keep several versions of your resume, for example one for each type of job.

Resume and cover letter tips

Keep your resume to one page when you can, start each achievement with an action word, and add numbers where you have them. Use words from the job ad in your skills and summary, and match your cover letter to the same design as your resume.
